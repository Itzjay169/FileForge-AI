# Page Design Override: About Us (`/about`)

## Purpose
Cinematic storytelling and transparent introduction to FileForge AI, its philosophy, free-forever commitment, and its creator **Vexora Technology** led by **Jaykishan Das (Jay)**.

## Layout & Motion Overrides
- **Hero:** Asymmetric split hero with bold typography, live particle ambient layer, and monospaced feature telemetry.
- **Story Section:** Staggered panel-style layout (manga-panel cadence) with ink borders.
- **Creator Section:** Dedicated card container with glowing border, social chips (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram), and editable profile parameters.
- **CTA:** Sticky bottom prompt with `<sakuga-button>` ("Open Creative Studio").
